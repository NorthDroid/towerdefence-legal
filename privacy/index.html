Tower Defense: Total Mayhem Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Tower Defense: Total Mayhem is a tower defence game made by northdroid. This policy explains what information the game handles. The short version: we do not collect personal data ourselves and you do not need an account to play, but the game shows ads from Google AdMob between levels, and Google collects some data to show them.

Information we collect

Tower Defense: Total Mayhem does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and we have no servers of our own that receive data about you. Gameplay statistics are described under Game analytics below.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, such as the levels you have completed and your settings, is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

The game sometimes shows an ad between levels, after a level you have won. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with the ads.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow tracking when the game asks. You can change this at any time in the iPhone's Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you decline, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Game analytics

To learn which levels are too hard and where players stop, the game sends gameplay events, such as a level started or finished, to Google Analytics for Firebase. Firebase also collects crash reports and basic device and app information. The events carry a random identifier for this installation of the game, so they are pseudonymous rather than anonymous, but they are not linked to your name, account or advertising identifier. This data is used only to improve the game, never for advertising. Where the law asks for your consent, the identifier is only stored if you agree, and you can change your choice under Settings → Privacy choices. See Firebase's privacy information.

Technical information sent by the game engine

Tower Defense: Total Mayhem is built with the Unity game engine. Unity may automatically send anonymous technical information, such as the device model, operating system version and graphics hardware, to Unity Technologies to help improve the engine. This information does not identify you. See Unity's privacy policy for details.

Beta testing

If you test Tower Defense: Total Mayhem through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share information with us such as crash reports, device and usage data, and any feedback you choose to send. This is handled under Apple's privacy policy and the TestFlight terms.

Children

Tower Defense: Total Mayhem is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If the data the game handles changes, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top.
