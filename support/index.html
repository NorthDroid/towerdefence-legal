Tower Defense: Total Mayhem Support

Help with the game, and how to reach us.

Contact

Email appdeveloper@northdroid.com. Tell us your level, your iPhone model and what happened; it helps us find the problem.

How to play

Enemies march along a fixed road toward your base. Tap an empty build slot to place a tower, and tap a tower to upgrade it. Towers placed next to each other strengthen one another, so where you build matters as much as what you build. Each enemy that gets through costs a life; keep at least one until the last wave to win.

Common questions

I'm stuck on a level

Every level can be solved. Try a different opening: build around the bends in the road, and group towers that boost each other. You can also restart the level at any time.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not yet. Your levels and settings are saved only on your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

How do I start over?

Open Settings from the main menu and tap Reset all progress. This can't be undone.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings → Privacy choices in the game. For tracking, go to the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the privacy policy for what data is used.

The game runs slowly or gets warm

In Settings, turn off 120 Hz. It makes the game look smoother but uses more power.

Requirements

An iPhone with iOS 15 or later. No account or sign-in is needed, and the game plays offline. Ads need an internet connection.
